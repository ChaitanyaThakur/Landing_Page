About

Think simple. Work smarter.

I'm a Technical Project Manager with a background in software engineering, currently leading delivery at STL Digital. Before that, I spent three years at Radiance Technologies running cross-functional teams across engineering, training, delivery, and placement — setting quarterly OKRs, automating workflows, and taking on-time delivery from 78% to 98%.

My path into project management started on the engineering side — full-stack development, technical training, and mentoring — which is why I care as much about how a team grows as I do about the roadmap. I regularly coach individuals adopting agile practices, and I hold a Master of Technology in Computer Software Engineering.

Outside of day-to-day delivery, I spend time mentoring early-career and transitioning tech professionals — from students figuring out where to start, to engineers moving into project management or agile roles.
